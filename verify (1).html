WHAT THIS PROVES

That the artwork follows from the historical record rather than being attached to it afterwards, and that nothing has been altered since publication.

WHAT IT DOESN'T

That a source is itself correct. Only reading the source can tell you that, and the link is right there on every record. If you find a date that disagrees with its source, please open an issue — a corrected record is worth more than an uncontested one.
